# Reckon HRMS: User manual script

Screen-recording steps with English voice-over. The Bangla voice-over is in docs/USER_MANUAL_BANGLA.md. Each scene lists what to show on screen, what to click, and what to say. Record each scene separately; keep each under 3 minutes.

Before recording: sign in as an HR Manager, have one company with two branches, one Office Location per branch, about ten employees, one attendance month, and one salary run. Use the demo company (see "Demo data" below) so no real employee appears on screen.

---

## Scene 1: Welcome and the left menu

**Screen:** the Reckon HRMS home page. Show the left navigation from the top.

**Click / show:** point at the first two items: HR Studio and Payroll Studio. Then Dashboard, People, Time & Attendance, Leave & Expense, Payroll, HR Reports and Payroll Reports.

**Voice-over:**
"Welcome to Reckon HRMS. Everything starts in the left menu. HR Studio covers the whole employee life cycle, from hiring to exit. Payroll Studio covers salary. The dashboard shows the numbers you need every day, and the reports are grouped at the bottom."


---

## Scene 2: Office locations for each branch

**Screen:** left menu, People section, then Office Location.

**Click / show:**
1. Open Office Location. Show the list: one row for each branch.
2. Open one row. Show Company, Branch, Latitude, Longitude and Radius in metres.
3. Point out that the branch field decides which location an employee checks in at.

**Voice-over:**
"Each branch has its own office location. Set the branch, the latitude and longitude, and the radius in metres. When an employee checks in, the system uses the location of their branch. If a branch has no location, the company's first location is used."


---

## Scene 3: Hiring

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Hiring".

**Click / show:**
1. Open HR Studio, then the Hiring step.
2. Open Job openings, then add a new opening.
3. Open Job applicants, then add an applicant.
4. Open Job offers, then add an offer and submit it.

**Voice-over:**
"Hiring starts with a job opening. Add an applicant against it. When the candidate accepts, record a job offer. Each step keeps the record, so nothing is lost between recruiter and HR."


---

## Scene 4: Company and employee setup

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Company and employee setup".

**Click / show:**
1. Show the lists: offices, departments, job titles, levels, types of employee.
2. Click the add icon next to Departments. Quick entry opens; add one and save. The list refreshes.
3. Show the import of people from a CSV file.

**Voice-over:**
"Set up the company once: offices, departments, job titles, levels and types of employee. Each list has an open icon and an add icon. To add many people, upload the template and check it before anything is saved."


---

## Scene 5: Onboarding a new employee

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Onboarding".

**Click / show:**
1. Open Onboarding templates. Show one template with its activities.
2. Open Employee onboarding and start one for a new joiner from the template.
3. Show how the checklist is ticked off.

**Voice-over:**
"When someone joins, start an onboarding checklist from a template. It lists the documents and tasks for the first days, and each task has an owner. When the checklist is complete, the employee is fully set up."


---

## Scene 6: Check in and out

**Screen:** left menu, Time & Attendance, "Check in / out". Use a phone-sized view if possible.

**Click / show:**
1. Open Check in / out as an employee. Show the Check in button and today's status.
2. Press Check in. Show the confirmation with the time and the branch location.
3. Show what happens outside the radius: a message gives the distance.

**Voice-over:**
"Employees check in and out from this page only. The system checks that they are within the radius of their branch's office. Outside the radius, the check-in is refused and the screen shows how far they are."


---

## Scene 7: Attendance and late or early marks

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Time and attendance", then Attendance.

**Click / show:**
1. Open Attendance and filter to this month. Show present, absent and late marks.
2. Open Shift Type. Show the start time and the grace period.

**Voice-over:**
"Attendance is built from check-ins and the shift timings. A late entry or an early exit is marked on the day. Those marks feed the late and early deduction that HR approves in the salary run."


---

## Scene 8: Leave and holidays

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Leave and holidays".

**Click / show:**
1. Open Leave types. Show casual, sick and earned leave.
2. Open Leave applications. Show one application and approve it.
3. Open Holiday lists. Show this year's list and the weekly off.

**Voice-over:**
"Set the leave types and the holiday calendar once. Employees apply for leave, and the manager approves it. Holidays are taken from the calendar, so attendance and pay follow it."


---

## Scene 9: Set the salary rules

**Screen:** left menu, Payroll Studio, step "Salary rules".

**Click / show:**
1. Show the tax years list and the pay items list, with the open and add icons.
2. Show the salary structures list.
3. Open the Payslip preview. Pick a structure and enter a base salary.
4. Point at the line under each amount: it shows how the amount is worked out, such as "60% of base salary".

**Voice-over:**
"In Payroll Studio, Salary rules holds the pay items and the salary structures. Pick a structure and a base salary, and the payslip preview shows each amount and how it is worked out. Nothing is saved from the preview."


---

## Scene 10: Run salary, step by step

**Screen:** Payroll Studio, step "Run salary".

**Click / show:**
1. Step 1: choose the month and the pay date. Tick "Include overtime" and "Include festival bonus" if they apply. Click Find employees.
2. Step 2: Check the list. Show the basic pay, one column per pay item, total additions, total deductions and gross. Show the late and early deduction section below the list: tick the people and Apply.
3. Change one amount: type a new value, enter the reason in the dialog, and send it for approval. Show the change waiting for approval.
4. Step 3: Create draft slips. Show the progress.
5. Click Preview draft slips. Show the table with a column for each pay item. Click the Payslip PDF icon on one row.
6. Show the Consolidated Salary Sheet PDF, which opens in a new tab.
7. Step 4: Submit slips.
8. Step 5: pick the bank account, enter the reference, and record the payment. Show the bank file and the hold list.

**Voice-over:**
"Running salary is five steps. First, choose the month and the people. Second, check the list: every pay item is there, with the totals beside it. Any amount can be changed, but a change needs a reason and an approval from another manager. Third, create the draft slips and preview them. Fourth, submit the slips. Fifth, pay. Nothing is paid until the last step."


---

## Scene 11: Payslips and the salary sheet

**Screen:** Payroll Studio, Run salary, the preview table.

**Click / show:**
1. Click the Payslip PDF icon. Show the payslip: company, month, employee summary, net pay box, paid and LOP days, earnings and deductions with year to date, total net payable and the amount in words.
2. Go back and open the Consolidated Salary Sheet. Show the header, the groups TOTAL GROSS SALARY and TOTAL DEDUCTIONS, and the legend of codes.
3. After payment, show the title change from Draft to Paid statement.

**Voice-over:**
"Each employee gets a payslip with the net pay, the paid days, the deductions and the year-to-date figures. The consolidated salary sheet lists everyone for the month, in landscape. Before payment it is marked Draft; after payment it becomes the salary statement."


---

## Scene 12: Exit

**Screen:** HR Studio, step "Exit".

**Click / show:**
1. Open Employee separation. Add a separation for a leaving employee with the last working day.
2. Open Exit interviews and show a record linked to the separation.
3. Show the final settlement in the next salary run as an additional salary.

**Voice-over:**
"When someone leaves, record the separation with the last working day. Hold the exit interview, then settle the final pay in the next salary run. The employee's record stays for the reports."


---

## Scene 13: The dashboard and reports

**Screen:** Dashboard, then HR Reports and Payroll Reports.

**Click / show:**
1. Show the sections in order: HR analytics, HR and attendance, KPI overview, payroll.
2. Open Monthly Attendance Sheet and Salary Register.

**Voice-over:**
"The dashboard opens with HR analytics, then attendance, the key figures and payroll. Every number links to the list behind it. The reports are grouped for HR and for payroll."


---

## Scene 14: Closing

**Screen:** the dashboard.

**Voice-over:**
"That is the full cycle: hire, onboard, record attendance and leave, run and pay salary, and exit. Every step keeps its record, so the reports always match. Thank you for watching."


---

## Demo data

The steps above need a demo company with:
- two branches, each with an Office Location;
- about ten employees with branches, departments and job titles;
- one month of attendance with a few late and early marks;
- one salary run that is paid, one hiring record, one onboarding and one separation.

Create it in a separate demo company, not in a live company. The demo data is set up once and then used for every recording.
