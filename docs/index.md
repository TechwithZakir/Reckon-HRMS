# Reckon HRMS: compact HR and payroll for small businesses

Reckon HRMS is a company-wise HR and payroll product from Reckon Technologies Ltd. It is designed for small businesses that want employee records, attendance, leave and payroll in one simple workspace, delivered as a subscription through [ReckonERP](https://app.reckon.tech).

## Detailed features

### Employees
- Central employee records for each company
- Designed so a business sees only its own company data

### Attendance and shifts
- Shift setup and attendance tracking
- A foundation for payroll that reflects days worked

### Leave
- Leave types, applications and balances
- Keeps leave and attendance connected to payroll

### Payroll
- Payroll processing for small teams
- Salary structures and payroll runs on a proven HR engine

### Company-wise SaaS design
- Each subscribing business is a separate tenant with its own company
- Starting data is created per company and never copied from another tenant
- Plugs into the Reckon SaaS Platform for signup, plans, payments and invoices
- Has its own admin role and module roles

## Built on Frappe HR

Frappe HR provides the HR and payroll engine. Reckon adds the company-wise SaaS layer, simpler workspace and Bangladesh-focused delivery.

## Who it is for

Small businesses and growing teams in Bangladesh that want to move HR and payroll off spreadsheets without adopting a heavy system.
